These options add branches, not removals. Declining a provider/live integration
keeps all local preparation, records and manual handoffs available. It avoids
setup and outbound spending for now; it does not bypass permissions, security,
rights, legal obligations or actual deployment verification.

The local file-record validator checks structural constraints and selected
credential fields. It does not replace a full secret scanner, legal review or
cryptographic verifier. Keep its “structure valid” outcome separate from
“authorized,” “signed,” “owned,” “cleared,” “backed” or “settled.”
