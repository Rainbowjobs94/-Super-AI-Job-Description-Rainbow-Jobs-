# Expanded v1.3 domain workflow menu

The original 01–07 templates remain present. Added templates 08–20:

08 deployment review; 09 environment audit; 10 standard/deep research;
11 IP-security/provenance; 12 copyright/license; 13 trademark search brief;
14 patent prior-art brief; 15 legal/compliance matrix; 16 debugging;
17 protocol review; 18 file-format design; 19 role/access matrix;
20 market analysis/trends.

All are Manual Trigger → Edit sample input → Prepare bounded output, with
inactive state and no HTTP, schedule, webhook, shell, credential or signer node.
Use public/synthetic metadata only. An approval boolean cannot enable execution.
Jurisdiction is required before jurisdiction-dependent legal conclusions;
these templates merely identify that hold.

The environment/IP templates audit metadata and planned controls, not actual
secret values or confidential research contents. Provider and instance privacy
must be reviewed separately. Error/output history may have instance-specific
behavior; no confidentiality guarantee follows from importing JSON.

Copyright/license, trademark and patent outputs are distinct checklists, not
ownership/clearance opinions or filings. Market outputs are research briefs, not
live data or financial advice. Deployment outputs require a later exact
authorization and actual target/health checks before being called deployed.
