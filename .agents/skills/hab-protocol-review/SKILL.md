# Protocol reconciliation

Identify token name, symbol, chain, address, decimals, cap and authority from
actual code and live read-only evidence. Keep MC/MCL/MJC and RC/SC aliases
unresolved until evidence supports equivalence. Distinguish mining KEM from ML-KEM.

Separate activity evidence, accounting credits, minted tokens, reserves,
obligations, collateral, market price and settlement. Human acknowledgment may
be recorded without asserting collateral, redeemability or monetary value.
Do not certify a peg, tax deduction, legal enforceability or zero-knowledge proof.

Inventory deployed roles before adding any pool, vault, treasury or supply.
Candidate registry deduplication is not chain verification. No mainnet writes,
custody, signing, autonomous market actions or new deployment without approval.

For smart contracts: inspect versioned source, compile, test caps and authority,
check replay/reentrancy/signature domains, simulate testnets, and require
independent audit and relevant qualified review before financial use.
