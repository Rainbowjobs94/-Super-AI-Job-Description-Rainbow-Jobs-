# Revision and compatibility refresh

Follow `docs/REVISION_SYNC.md` in the installed toolkit. Inventory the actual
default branch, relevant unmerged branches, current PR head and Replit checkout
independently. Keep complete hashes, observation dates and access status.

Use `reconcile` for bounded offline comparison. It creates a review plan only;
there is no autonomous cross-project synchronizer or deployment authority.

Do not conflate the portable Hab operator with a project's designated consensus
consolidation hub. Preserve separate scopes and existing services.

Preserve project-specific skill modules with their references, source hashes,
license notices and adapters. Names alone do not establish identical versions.
Private source overlays must stay owner-private; unreviewed rights are not
permission for public redistribution or upload to another AI service.

Review new economic proposals and deployment candidates without silently
renaming tokens, changing decimals, granting mint authority or relaxing holds.
Source-based values, payout targets and version-selection plans are not live
price, backing, safety, audit or settlement evidence.
